History

Ferrari is one of the most iconic luxury sports car brands in the world. Founded in 1939 by Enzo Ferrari in Italy, the company became famous for producing high-performance supercars and dominating Formula 1 racing.

Ferrari represents speed, innovation, and elegant Italian design. Over the decades, Ferrari has created legendary cars that combine advanced engineering with breathtaking performance.
